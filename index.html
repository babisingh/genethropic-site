our biggest project right now
ArtGene Archive

A public archive for sequences that nature never wrote.

Generative models now design proteins, genes and regulatory elements faster than the literature can keep track of them. ArtGene Archive catalogues them in one public place, and every sequence is screened for biosafety before it is listed.

Three tools you can open today

read it

Gene-Story

What if all human genes could tell their stories? All 62,700 of them!

Read the genome like a book. Each chromosome is a chapter, and every gene, pseudogene and lncRNA explains its part in making you, you.

Open Gene-Story
ask it

Gene-Intel

Find a gene's functional twin in a wildly different species.

Ask in plain language. Gene-Intel compares how genes are built and who their neighbours are, across 15 species and five kingdoms of life.

Open Gene-Intel
map it

Gene-Maps

Look up a gene's address in 3D, like checking a place on a map before you visit.

The genome folds up like a bowl of ramen. See a gene's neighbours, how risky a CRISPR edit would be, and how druggable it looks.

Open Gene-Maps

On the bench, and coming next

on the bench

TINSEL

Foresight-funded

Lets protein-design tools check, sign and trace every sequence they generate: biosafety gates, a watermark hidden in synonymous codons, and tamper-evident post-quantum signatures.

BioClaw

Proof of concept

AI agents that reproduce a lab protocol end to end. Give it a protein accession, get back a checked plasmid design for E. coli. First example: insulin.
